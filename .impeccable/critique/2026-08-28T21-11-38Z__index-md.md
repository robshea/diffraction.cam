## Design Specificity Verdict

Genuinely specific, not a reskinned template. The colspan-grouped f-stop x wavelength grid, double-coded by wavelength band and diffraction rating, is the product. The Camera Details panel's Safe/Caution/Avoid triad is a second well-reasoned, product-specific touch. Deterministic scan ran clean but in degraded regex-fallback mode (htmlparser2/css-select/css-tree/domutils unavailable) — treat "clean" as "the weak fallback found nothing," not a clean bill of health. Contrast numbers were manually sampled live instead and are trustworthy: all three rating states clear WCAG AA.

## Overall Impression

The core mechanism is well-built and its best idea (Safe/Caution/Avoid) is genuinely excellent. But there's a real, source-confirmed edge-case bug in the interaction most likely to be triggered by an idle click, and DESIGN.md documents sticky leading columns that were never implemented — the table needs horizontal scroll even at ~1790px desktop width. Both fixable quickly; neither degrades the common-case experience.

## What's Working

1. The Safe/Caution/Avoid triad — turns a 110-cell grid into one confident, color-matched sentence.
2. Contrast and focus, both now solid: .good 5.20:1, .maybe 4.84:1, .bad 4.86:1, all clear AA; visible focus ring on every interactive element tabbed to. Both were the previous critique's top complaints; both are now fixed.
3. URL-persisted state works cleanly for the happy path.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Re-selecting the placeholder camera option writes an unrecoverable value into the shareable URL**
Why it matters: main.js's updatePitch() reads the selected option's .label unconditionally and writes it into ?camera=. The placeholder option is a normal, selectable list item — clicking it produces ?camera=-+select+camera+-, which can't re-resolve on reload.
Fix: skip the URL write when the selected value is empty; delete the camera param instead.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] The table's leading f-stop column isn't actually sticky, contradicting DESIGN.md**
Why it matters: DESIGN.md states "two frozen leading columns"; no position:sticky exists in main.scss. Measured real overflow even at ~1790px desktop width (scrollWidth 1230 vs clientWidth 1192), and scrolling right loses the row label off-screen entirely.
Fix: add position:sticky;left:0 with an opaque background to the two leading columns, or correct DESIGN.md's "frozen" language if sticky was never the intent.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P2] No error messaging when a ?camera= link doesn't match any camera**
Why it matters: loadDefaultCamera() silently leaves the placeholder selected on a mismatch. The resulting empty state is legible and non-broken, but gives no diagnosis of the stale-link failure mode.
Fix: detect the mismatch and surface an inline .notice--warning naming the camera that wasn't found.
Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] Camera Details panel's 9 fields have no grouping or hierarchy**
Why it matters: Camera, Filter, Safe, Caution, Avoid, Pitch, Megapixels, Pixels, Sensor render as one flat, identically-weighted row, burying the single most decision-relevant line (Safe/Caution/Avoid) next to Sensor dimensions.
Fix: give Safe/Caution/Avoid a visually distinct sub-cluster separate from the spec readouts.
Suggested command: /impeccable layout

**[P3] Several touch targets fall under the 44x44px guideline**
Why it matters: measured live: f-stop row hit area 29.5x42.75px, Help icon link 27.5x33px, "thirds" toggle link 40.8x19.5px.
Fix: pad the row-label cell and the two small links closer to 44px in their constrained dimension.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**: Only path to instructions is a small ? icon next to the Filter field; nothing proactively explains filter-driven column graying before the fact.

## Minor Observations

- No visual affordance signals more columns exist off-screen (no fade edge, no "scroll for more" hint).
- Filter select defaults to "Full spectrum" regardless of loaded camera, showing results for a spectrum many visitors can't shoot until they change it.
- Cameras page table shows the same right-edge clipping pattern at the same viewport.
- Caption sits below the table per DESIGN.md's deliberate convention, though it also functions as the page's only "what am I looking at" label.
